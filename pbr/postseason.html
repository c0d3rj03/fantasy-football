🧮 Postseason Bonus

Every team plays inside its post-Q3 division. There are no head-to-head games: weekly scores add up across Weeks 13-17, and the lowest running total is cut. Each team starts with a head start, added to its Week 13 score on MFL as the "Postseason Bonus", based on its full regular season (Weeks 1-12).

Upper Division · rewards the best teams
avg = Season PF ÷ 12
midpoint = average of the 4 teams' avg
bonus = 5 × (avg − midpoint), only if above the midpoint
Middle & Lower · helps the thinner rosters
avg = Season Potential Points ÷ 12
top = the division's best avg
bonus = 5 × (top − avg), 0 for the top team
